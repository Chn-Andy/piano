XinQin is a duel in ink.

Notes fall with the music and you play them on letter keys laid out like a real piano — every note you hit turns into a sword-qi strike that cuts down the ink fiends pouring out of the battlefield. They advance on your line in packs and, once they arrive, bite you every two beats: a miss never costs health directly, but your blade whiffs, your damage drops, and the tide creeps closer. Combos raise your damage, chords are two-handed heavy blows; at the end of each phrase the Resonance fires by itself, cashing in whatever you just played — the better the phrase, the wilder the ink. When the song peaks, a vermilion Finale seal drops and you settle it with the Ink Sovereign.

How it plays

· Three public-domain pieces × three difficulties: Canon in D / Für Elise / Moonlight on the Spring River. Easy starts at 7 white keys, Medium adds black keys (12), Hard widens to a two-row 20-key hand position.
· Judgement: press as the note crosses the judgement line. Perfect ±50 ms / Good ±120 ms; a miss breaks the combo (no health loss — damage comes only from fiends biting at your line).
· Hold the line: fiends advance from deep in the field to your line, then bite for 10 HP every two beats. Sword-qi is a full-field AoE that decays with depth — every hit strikes everything, front rank takes it all, the back rank less. Lose all health and you lose; survive to the end of the song with the field cleared (Ink Sovereign included) and you win.
· Tinted notes (about 6%): Frost (knocks the field back and slows it), Venom (poisons the front fiend), Blast (heavy hit on the front two) — placed deterministically in the chart, so the same song always lays out the same way.
· Heartstring: combos charge it; a full gauge releases a field-wide burst of clarity on its own, with no extra key to press.
· Resonance: fires automatically at each phrase end, scaled by how well you played that phrase.
· Rating: S / A / B / C, with your best run saved locally so you can come back and beat yourself.
· Pure web page, play in the browser, nothing to install; includes audio/visual offset calibration and separate music / SFX volume.

Keys

· White keys: A S D F J K L (one octave, C4–B4)
· Black keys: W E T Y U (between the white keys, like a real piano)
· Hard widens the range: I O P upwards, Z X C downwards
· Esc pauses / goes back; ↑↓ picks a song, ←→ picks a difficulty, Enter starts the duel

Desktop keyboard only (no touch support).

Language: the interface is available in 中文 and English — it follows your browser language and can be switched at any time from the library badge or the settings screen.
